Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version. What you write in The Crossing is yours. We store it so the app works for you. Nobody at Indigo Estate reads your journal. We never sell your information or use it for advertising. You can download everything or delete your account yourself, at any time, from Witness → Your account.

Who we are

The Crossing is run by Life and Soul Place Ltd, trading as Indigo Estate, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16827306). We are the "controller" of your personal information, which means we decide how it is used and are responsible for keeping it safe. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

To ask anything about your information, or to use any of your rights below, email enquiries@indigoestate.co.uk.

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The app stores a sign-in token on your device so you stay signed in. That's strictly necessary, so it doesn't need a cookie banner.

Sensitive information

What you write may include details about your health, your religious or spiritual beliefs, your family and your past. The law calls this "special category" information and protects it more strongly. We only store it because you explicitly agree to that when you create your account, and only so the app can work for you. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account, which deletes your journal.

The Mirror (AI reflections)

The Mirror is optional. It only runs when you press a Mirror button. When you do, the parts of your journal needed for that reflection are sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, to write the reply. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, information sent through its service isn't used to train its AI models. It may be kept for a limited time for safety and abuse checks. If you'd rather nothing leaves the app's own storage, simply don't use the Mirror; everything else works without it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and journal: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your journal, reminders and account from our live systems straight away.
  • Reminder sign-ups: until you stop reminders, unsubscribe from an email, or delete your account.
  • Mirror usage counts: kept with your account and deleted with it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • See your information. Use Download my journal, or email us for a full copy.
  • Correct it. Edit anything in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete it. Use Delete my account, or ask us.
  • Take it elsewhere. The download is a plain text file you can keep.
  • Withdraw consent to reminders or to storing your journal, at any time.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your information.

We'll reply within one month. There's no charge.

Who the app is for

The Crossing is for adults aged 18 and over. It's a reflection and accountability practice, not therapy or medical care. If something you're working through feels like too much, please talk to someone you trust, or call Samaritans free on 116 123 (UK, any time).

Keeping it safe

All information travels over encrypted connections. Your journal can only be loaded by your own signed-in account; the app's code never returns one member's journal to anyone else. Passwords are stored in scrambled form that can't be read back, even by us.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first at enquiries@indigoestate.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email first.
